# Universal Super-Knowledge Ontology

Listed on GAIA 2.0. Not a live reasoner. Super knowledge is not Super Intelligence.

Twelve layers, SK-01 through SK-12: information, knowledge, context, provenance, evidence, uncertainty, relationships, causality, systems, cross-domain synthesis, emergent knowledge, wisdom.

Checked load: 12 layers, 149 layer items, 14 perspectives, 10 unknown kinds, 6 claim statuses, 6 distinctions, 6 causal grades.

A perspective records a standpoint. It does not decide truth. Emergence is a hypothesis until evidence updates the claim. Causal grades stay separate: co-occurs, contributes, causes, necessary, sufficient, conditional.

Super-skills in this cut are the five operations named for the next ontology: integrate, compose, adapt, verify, improve. That is not a universal skill catalog.

The learning loop is subject, knowledge, super-knowledge, skill, action, observation, evidence, updated knowledge.

Rule: no row may collapse observed, inferred, hypothesized, believed, unknown, and verified into one status.
