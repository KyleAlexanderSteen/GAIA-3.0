# Universal Super-Power and Super-Capability Ontology

Listed on GAIA 2.0. Super power does not mean supernatural. It means a measurable expansion of action-space, and none of these rows are measured here.

SC-01 through SC-20 are super capabilities. SP-01 through SP-08 are families. Every power in this seed is conceptual. Conceptual is not possible, possible is not demonstrated, demonstrated is not operational.

Constraints required before action: authorization, safety, ethics, reversibility, evidence, uncertainty, physical limit, computational limit, and the refusal to treat consciousness as an axiom.

Platforms are human, AI, SI, organization, and machine. A power can belong to a system. No platform is marked conscious.
