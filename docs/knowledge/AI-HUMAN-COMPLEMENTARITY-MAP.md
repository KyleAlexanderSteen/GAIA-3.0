## Overview

The AI-Human Complementarity Map defines, for every human skill domain, the relationship between human capability and AI capability — specifically:

1. **What AI does that amplifies the human** (augmentation)
2. **What remains irreducibly human** (the permanent human zone)
3. **Where the boundary sits today** (and how it may shift)

This map is the operational expression of GAIA 2.0's core value: **AI and humans are complementary, not competitive.** It is used by GAIAN twins to frame their AI assistance in ways that enhance human agency rather than erode it.

---

## Complementarity Framework

Every skill maps to one of four complementarity zones:

| Zone | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Zone 1** | Human Irreplaceable | Skill is permanently human — embodied, biological, relational, or experiential by definition |
| **Zone 2** | Human Primary, AI Augments | Human judgment and creativity lead; AI provides speed, breadth, and recall |
| **Zone 3** | Collaborative Frontier | Human and AI co-create; neither alone reaches the peak |
| **Zone 4** | AI Capable, Human Oversight | AI can perform the task; human oversight, ethics, and final call remain essential |

> **Note:** Zone 4 does not mean humans are replaced. It means humans govern. No GAIAN twin ever operates in Zone 4 without active human oversight.

## The Permanent Human Zone

Of the 81 skills in the HSD, the complementarity analysis finds:

- **Zone 1 (Human Irreplaceable):** 54 skills (67%) — permanently human by nature
- **Zone 2 (Human Primary, AI Augments):** 15 skills (18%) — humans lead, AI supports
- **Zone 3 (Collaborative Frontier):** 9 skills (11%) — genuine human-AI co-creation
- **Zone 4 (AI Capable, Human Oversight):** 3 skills (4%) — AI can perform, human governs

**67% of all human skills are permanently irreplaceable by AI.** This is GAIA 2.0's empirical foundation for its human-first philosophy.
