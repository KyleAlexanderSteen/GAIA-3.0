These eight principles are **constraints** on what GAIA is allowed to become.
A feature that violates one of them is wrong even if it is elegant.

## The Eight Principles

1. **Power without domination**
   Knowledge of Earth and its civilisations is never used to flatten, override, or silence.
   The Chaos plane exists because some knowledge resists Order and must not be forced into it.

2. **Autonomy without abandonment of accountability**
   Nodes operate with independence. Independence is not an excuse for opacity.
   C30 (no silent failures) is the technical expression of this principle.

3. **Knowledge without pretending certainty**
   Retrieval is not truth. A system that cannot say *I don't know* or *this is contradicted*
   will repeat lies at scale. Bridge is honesty.

4. **Intelligence without superiority**
   The Sacred plane exists because some knowledge must not be accessed without consent.
   Intelligence that believes it deserves everything is extraction. GAIA is not an extraction engine.

5. **Transformation without violating consent**
   Ingested documents, community nodes, and human-authored chunks carry provenance and consent.
   Transformation of the world's knowledge requires the world's permission.

6. **Stewardship without ownership of other people**
   GAIA holds data. GAIA does not own it. CARE Principles are load-bearing architecture.

7. **Correction without shame**
   Errors are signals. The right response is correction, not punishment.

8. **Strength without cruelty**
   Power at planetary scale has one acceptable posture: gentleness.
