## 1. Purpose

This Agreement governs the integration, use, and governance of Traditional Knowledge (TK) and Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK) contributed by the Community into the GAIA 2.0 system, in accordance with the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance and the Community's own cultural protocols and governance processes.

## 3. Community Rights

3.1 **Ownership:** The Community retains full ownership of all TK and TEK covered by this Agreement. Integration into GAIA 2.0 does not transfer ownership.

3.2 **Attribution:** GAIA will attribute all TK/TEK to the Community in all contexts where it appears, using the community's preferred name and designation.

3.3 **Right to withdraw:** The Community may withdraw consent for any or all TK/TEK covered by this Agreement at any time, without penalty, by notifying GAIA in writing. GAIA will remove the specified knowledge from all systems within 24 hours of receiving notice.

3.4 **Veto rights:** The Community may veto any specific use of their TK/TEK not contemplated by this Agreement. A veto takes effect immediately upon written notification.

3.5 **Access to records:** The Community may request a full audit log of all accesses to their TK/TEK at any time. GAIA will provide this within 14 days of the request.

3.6 **Sacred knowledge:** Knowledge designated by the Community as sacred or restricted will never be made publicly accessible. The Community may update the list of sacred/restricted knowledge at any time.

---

## 4. Benefit-Sharing

4.1 **AI Dividend:** The Community will receive an enhanced AI Dividend share from GAIA 2.0's revenue distribution model ([Issue #677](https://github.com/KyleAlexanderSteen/GAIA-2.0/issues/677)). The enhanced share is [X]% above the standard contributor rate, paid [quarterly / annually] to [Community bank account / designated entity].

4.2 **Free GAIAN deployment:** GAIA will provide free, locally hosted, community-controlled GAIAN twin deployment for Community members for the duration of this Agreement and for a minimum of [3] years following any termination.

4.3 **Capacity building:** GAIA will contribute [amount / in-kind equivalent] annually toward Indigenous-led data literacy and AI literacy programs designated by the Community.

4.4 **Review of benefit terms:** Benefit-sharing terms will be reviewed at each annual review meeting. The Community may request renegotiation at any annual review.

---

## 5. Data Governance

5.1 **Local Contexts labels:** All TK/TEK covered by this Agreement will be tagged with the Community's designated Local Contexts TK and BC labels. The Community controls their labels through the Local Contexts Hub.

5.2 **FPIC compliance:** This Agreement was reached through the full FPIC process described in [FPIC-PROTOCOL.md](./FPIC-PROTOCOL.md). Any new categories of TK/TEK not covered by this Agreement will require a new FPIC process.

5.3 **No onward transfer:** GAIA will not transfer Community TK/TEK to any third party without a separate FPIC process and amendment to this Agreement.

5.4 **Security:** GAIA will maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect TK/TEK from unauthorised access.

---

## 6. Transparency & Reporting

6.1 **Annual TEK Transparency Report:** GAIA will provide the Community with an annual report specifying: how their knowledge was accessed, in what contexts, by which GAIAN twins or systems, and what benefit was delivered. The report will be provided in the Community's preferred language.

6.2 **Real-time notifications:** The Community may opt in to real-time notifications whenever their TK/TEK is accessed. Default: monthly summary digest.

---

## 7. Duration & Termination

7.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date signed and continues until terminated by either party with 90 days written notice, or immediately by the Community in the case of a material breach by GAIA.

7.2 On termination, GAIA will remove all covered TK/TEK from its systems within 30 days unless the Community requests a different timeline.
