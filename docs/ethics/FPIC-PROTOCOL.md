## Overview

Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC) is an Indigenous right established under the UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (UNDRIP, Article 19 and 32). No Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK) or cultural knowledge from any Indigenous community may be integrated into any GAIA 2.0 system without completing this protocol in full.

**Free** — Consent is given voluntarily, without coercion, manipulation, or undue influence. No payment or benefit may be offered as inducement to consent.

**Prior** — Consent is sought and obtained *before* any TEK collection, integration, or use begins — not retroactively.

**Informed** — The community receives complete, accurate, plain-language information about what data will be collected, how it will be used, who will access it, what benefits they will receive, and what their rights are.

## FPIC Process — Six Stages

### Stage 1 — Initial Contact & Relationship Building

- GAIA 2.0 team makes contact through appropriate community channels (not bypassing governance structures)
- Relationship building precedes any formal request — minimum 3 months of engagement before FPIC process begins
- Cultural protocols observed: introductions, gift exchange norms, language preferences
- Community designates their FPIC liaison(s) — GAIA 2.0 works only through designated liaisons
- No data collection of any kind during this stage

### Stage 2 — Information Sharing

- GAIA 2.0 provides full written disclosure (in community's preferred language) covering:
  - Exactly what TEK is being requested
  - How it will be stored, tagged, and protected
  - Who can access it and under what conditions
  - What benefit-sharing the community will receive
  - The community's right to withdraw at any time
  - How sacred/restricted knowledge will be protected
  - The Local Contexts TK/BC label system and how it works
- Community has minimum **60 days** to review, consult internally, and seek independent advice
- GAIA 2.0 provides access to independent legal and technical advisors at no cost to the community

### Stage 3 — Community Deliberation

- The community deliberates according to their own governance processes — GAIA 2.0 does not participate in or observe this process
- Community may request additional information, modifications to the proposed agreement, or a site visit
- No timeline pressure — deliberation continues until the community is ready

### Stage 4 — Consent Decision

- Community communicates their decision through their designated liaison
- Decision options:
  - **Full consent** — proceed as proposed
  - **Conditional consent** — proceed with specified modifications (e.g. certain knowledge excluded, access restrictions added)
  - **Partial consent** — specific TEK categories approved, others excluded
  - **Refusal** — no TEK integration; GAIA 2.0 accepts this unconditionally and maintains the relationship
- Consent is documented in writing, signed by authorised community representatives
- Consent is **revocable at any time** — this is stated explicitly in all consent documents

### Stage 5 — Benefit-Sharing Agreement

- Before any TEK is collected, the formal benefit-sharing agreement is executed (see [BENEFIT-SHARING-TEMPLATE.md](./BENEFIT-SHARING-TEMPLATE.md))
- Agreement specifies: AI Dividend share, free GAIAN deployment, attribution terms, data withdrawal process, annual reporting obligations
- Agreement is reviewed by community's independent legal representative
- Both parties sign; agreement is publicly disclosed (unless community requests confidentiality)

### Stage 6 — Ongoing Consent & Review

- Consent is not a one-time event — it is an ongoing relationship
- Annual review meeting between GAIA 2.0 and community liaison
- Community receives annual TEK Transparency Report specific to their knowledge
- Community can modify, restrict, or withdraw consent at any review or at any time between reviews
- Community veto on any new use of their TEK not covered by the original agreement

## FPIC Non-Compliance

Any GAIA 2.0 contributor, operator, or GAIAN twin deployment that collects, uses, or integrates TEK without completing this FPIC protocol is in violation of GAIA 2.0's core governance policies. Violations trigger:

1. Immediate removal of the TEK from all GAIA 2.0 systems
2. Formal notification to the affected community
3. Review by the Multi-Stakeholder Council Ethics Committee
4. Potential suspension or removal of the violating contributor
5. Community offered remediation and compensation

---

## First Three Community Partnerships (Acceptance Criteria)

The first three community partnerships must complete the full FPIC process before the CARE Principles acceptance criteria are considered met. GAIA 2.0 will prioritise communities in regions with high ecological knowledge relevance to the Biological Layer ([Issue #661](https://github.com/KyleAlexanderSteen/GAIA-2.0/issues/661)):

1. **Amazon basin** — via Amazon Sacred Headwaters Alliance
2. **Pacific Islands** — via Pacific Community (SPC)
3. **Australia** — via AIATSIS
