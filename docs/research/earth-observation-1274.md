## Twin rung

NASA ESTO, October 2024, defines an Earth System Digital Twin as three capabilities: a replica of past and current state, forecasts from that replica, and what-if scenarios. Their roadmap at that date was prototypes, not an operational planetary twin.

GAIA's current rung is below that. The crate test is a fixture ensemble. It has no replica, no forecast from observations, and no scenario fed by a sensor. Maturity claim without a validation procedure stays Tier C.
