# Research-note quality checklist

For #1278 and #1282. A research pull request is not sufficient until each line is true.

- The authority is named, with a date. A standard, a paper, or a dataset. "The literature" is not an authority.
- The field that authority fills is named. A formula without the decomposition, or a dataset row without license and time range, fails.
- A witness row is marked witness. It is not cited as a harvested record.
- External numbers are attributed. They are not GAIA results.
- The note says what would make it sufficient: a real source row, a resolved horizon, or a license text.
- The cut adds no ingest, no trainer, and no client.

#1276 and #1277 are reviewed against this list before merge. Passing the list does not close #1171, #1272, #1273, #1274, or #1275.
