# PERSP-004 witness limit

Issue: #1257. Listed only. A report is not a finding.

What can be recorded: a sentence, a receipt, a trace header with flags `00`.
What cannot be verified from inside the system: that the reporter is conscious, that a tradition is true, that a conflict of goods was resolved rightly.

A witness eligible to judge a case stands outside the optimizer and outside the catalog. `EligibleForReview` is not that witness. This note does not close #1257.
