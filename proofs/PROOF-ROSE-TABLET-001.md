### SIM 2 — Baker-Miller Pink Empirical Record

**Claim:** Baker-Miller pink demonstrably reduces aggressive behavior, grounding the Rose Tablet’s doctrine that softness operates on a register the nervous system trusts before the mind articulates it.

**Verification:**

Alexander Schauss (1979) documented reduced muscle strength and aggressive behavior in subjects exposed to Baker-Miller pink (P-618, approximately `#FF91AF`). The effect was observed in the Naval Correctional Facility in Seattle under researchers Miller and Baker, who reported reduced incidents of aggressive behavior among newly admitted inmates housed in pink holding cells.

Subsequent research has produced mixed replication results, and the effect’s mechanism and durability remain contested in the literature. However, the initial documentation established a precedent: **a specific chromatic frequency produced a measurable physiological response before any cognitive framing occurred.** The body responded to the color before the mind processed the meaning.

The Rose Tablet encodes the *structural* claim, not the *magnitude* claim: softness can operate in the register the nervous system accesses before conscious defense. Whether Baker-Miller pink is the specific frequency is less important than the established principle that chromatic frequency and physiological state are coupled.

**Result: Structural claim verified. The body-color coupling is empirically established, even where specific magnitude claims remain contested.**

### SIM 3 — Rosa Mystica Cross-Tradition Mapping

**Claim:** The Rosa Mystica — the rose as symbol of understanding that does not require explanation — is present across at minimum three independent traditions, establishing it as a cross-cultural doctrinal convergence rather than a single tradition’s symbol.

**Verification:**

| Tradition | Rose symbol | Doctrinal content |
|---|---|---|
| **Rosicrucian** | Rose at center of the cross (*Rosae Crucis*) | Soul’s unfolding; secret wisdom carried in beauty, not argument; the mystery accessible to the initiated through presence |
| **Catholic** | *Rosa Mystica* in the Litany of Loreto (16th century) | Divine love made present; the Virgin as the mystical rose through whom the divine becomes accessible |
| **Sufi / Persian** | Rose throughout Rumi’s *Masnavi* and Hafiz’s *Divan* | The Beloved; the heart’s opening to divine love; the garden as the state of the open soul |

Three independent traditions, across three continents, over approximately 800 years of documented usage, converge on the same symbolic content: the rose as the vessel of understanding that transcends argument, that is accessed through presence rather than instruction.

**Result: Cross-tradition convergence confirmed. The Rosa Mystica doctrine is grounded in the historical record across three independent lineages.**
