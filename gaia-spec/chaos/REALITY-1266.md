# Reality and the good

Issues #1266 and #1267. First sourced pass. Leaves both open.

Quine, On What There Is (1948), treats existence as what a theory is committed to. Physical objects are posits of science, not names. Abstract objects, in Word and Object chapter 7, stay in the theory and are not a physical measure.

Nussbaum, Women and Human Development (2000), asks what a person is able to do and be, not how satisfied they are. Sen's capability approach is the same cut. Doyal and Gough name needs. None of these is a grant to decide the higher good for other people. Stewardship is not sovereignty.
