# EARTH-002 collective intelligence

Issue #1224. Research. Leaves the issue open.

Collective intelligence here means task performance. It is not collective consciousness. Woolley et al., Science 2010, reported a group factor. Lorenz et al., PNAS 2011, showed social influence can make a crowd worse. Nakagaki, Nature 2000, showed a slime mold solving a maze. A maze is not a mind.
