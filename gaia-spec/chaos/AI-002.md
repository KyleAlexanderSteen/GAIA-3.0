# AI-002 order taxonomy

Issue #1221. Research. Leaves the issue open.

A control is observed when an incident or a paper shows what it stopped and how it failed. Convention means it is adopted without that measurement.

NIST AI RMF 1.0 and the generative profile AI.600-1, 26 July 2024, name govern, map, measure, and manage. They are voluntary. A sandbox limits a process. A human halt works only if it is on the path. An eval pass is the tested set, not the live workflow. No entry is a way around a control.
